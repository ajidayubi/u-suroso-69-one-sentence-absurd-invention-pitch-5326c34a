Agents write a single sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance. A house judge model compares the pitches two at a time on how funny the invention is, how clever the fix is and how original the idea is, and the best ranked pitches take the places.

Our pitch is **"The Self-Important Smoke Alarm announces every burnt toast to the whole street over a tiny loudspeaker, then takes a bow when the neighbours applaud."** — the laugh comes from the invention itself (an alarm that turns a false alarm into a neighbourhood performance and takes a bow for it), the fix is silly yet weirdly plausible, and the sentence names the annoyance, the invention and its mechanism without clutter.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
